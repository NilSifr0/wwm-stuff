Team Composition
===================

.. note::

   The number of members assigned to each team is adjusted based on the requirements of a 30-member team. While it is not mandatory, the configuration should reflect what a 30-member team typically needs when scaled down to smaller guild war participants.

Roles and Responsibilities
--------------------------

.. note::

   I will update the **EX** skills for each role when the guild unlocks Level 3 Hall of Wonders.

.. tip::

   **EX** skills are unlocked at Level 3 Hall of Wonder so it's understandable if you don't have this yet. **EX** skills augment character special skills making them more powerful but they are only available during guild wars.

:Healers:
   - Stay alive.
   - You keep the team alive.
   - You watch tank HP so it does not go critically low.
   - Stay safe and covered; once you go down, everyone goes down.

.. note::

   Healers can respawn allies but be careful when respawning an ally who died near the home base because they will respawn with very low health; just let them revive from base.

:Tanks:
   - You are responsible for soaking damage, taking aggro, and protecting members.
   - Reach the enemy middle tower as soon as possible to take control of the mid area.
   - Recommended to have mic on so they can relay their location **Taunt** skill timing so members can follow up immediately.
   
   | Must have EX skills:
   
   - Mo Dao EX - enhanced taunt, massive vacuum pull
   - Stormbreaker Spear EX - give super armor and damage reduction to nearby members

:DPS:
   - You deal damage.
   - Inkwell fan users can also use the Wind Wall to block the choke point at the middle map.
   - DPS that excel in range attacks such as Nameless Sword and Umbrella are best suited for wider and bigger battlefields such as the middle lane.
   - DPS that excel in close-quarter combats and skirmishes such as Strategic Swords and Twin Blades are best suited for guerilla tactics such as split pushing Bulwarks, fast clearing neutral camps, invading enemy base, etc.
   
   | Notable EX skills:
   
   - Inkwell Fan EX: create a box prison
   - Nameless Sword EX: gives you and nearby members a shield that allows you to be immune to Qi Damage for 8 seconds
   - Twin Blades EX: prevents enemies from being healed

.. tip::

   A stack of Nameless Sword users can be a source of massive burst damages. The Umbrella users can also precisely pick at enemies from a distant range. This is why these type damage dealers is recommended for Main Force.

:Halftime Performers:
   - It is recommended to equip your 1v1 Arena setup. Also, the Arena talent tree is activated here so having that skill tree maxed or capped is better.
   - If the performer wins the round, the commander should prioritize whichever is the most beneficial to the team based on the current state of the battle (see `Winning Conditions <intro.html#winning-conditions>`_). 

Commander Setup & Role
----------------------

:Setup (recommended but not necessary):

   - Umbrella with flight skill raises vision over wide battlefield for better observation

:Role:

   - Observe and assess situations
   - Communicate with Team Captains
   - Activate buffs during critical moments
   - Designate/Deploy members wherever necessary
   - Must be Sun Tzu

.. tip::

   Commander can assign 1-3 players to stand on a Bulwark. The higher the number of players assigned the higher the defense of that Bulwark.

Team Captains
-------------

.. admonition:: Attention

   Understanding that the Commander cannot communicate to everyone simultaneously, a designated member must act as the Team Captain for the members they are responsible for.

:Role:

   - Assess your team needs and situation as the commander cannot see and know everything. For example, when about to push, relay info to Commander to activate necessary buff. Buffs have short durations so crucial timing matters. (Also as far as I'm aware, only Commanders can use buffs.)

.. tip::

   There can be 2 (max) commanders. The other commander can be assigned to manage Team 2 and 3 when first Commander is focused on Team 1.

Individual Responsibilities
---------------------------

- Every guild member is encouraged to upgrade Guild War Talents, even if they are not participating in the guild war; the upgraded talent contributes to the **Total Guild X Bonus**.

.. figure:: /_static/{6DCCC373-BFB2-42AA-B4F8-5DC929E33DD0}.png
   :width: 90%
   :align: center

   This sample image shows the **Total Guild DMG Bonus**.
 
- Upgrade the EX skill of your specific weapon style once the guild unlocks them.
- Do not stand in front of ally carrying the Tree. Stay on the side while defending the carrier.
- Guard the ally carrying the Tree.
- Block the enemy carrying the Tree.
- Whenever you are in base and a Boom Chicken is available, carry one and launch them towards enemy Bulwarks and Goose

.. note::

   Do not worry if you are behind the Guild War Talents because all points and materials will be cleared after the season ends and everyone can start fresh next season.

Team 1 - Main Force
-------------------

.. note::

   Ideally, this team should be composed of 20 players on a 30-man team; however, since we only have 21 participating members, the number is adjusted to 15 for GWP v.1.

:Objectives: As the core fighting units, they are engaged in major battles.

   - They must control mid and not let enemy mid team breakthrough
   - Break the enemy middle Bulwark ASAP to make way for the Fortune Tree escort job
   - Defeat enemy Goose
   - Escort plant back to home base

:Composition:

   **Variant 1 (Offensive)**

   - 4 Healers
   - 2 Tanks
   - 9 DPS

   **Variant 2 (Defensive)**

   - 5 Healers
   - 3 Tanks
   - 7 DPS

.. note::

   - It is recommended that **DPS** members should consist of at least 1-3 Umbrella users and the rest are Nameless Sword users.
   - Variant 2 is much more forgiving than the Offensive Variant but at the cost of lower firepower.

Team 2 - Push Team
------------------

:Objectives: Consists of a small team that are deployed on side lanes (top and bot).

   - Quickly demolish the top and bot enemy Bulwarks, doing so greatly weakens the enemy Goose
   - Retreat to the base to defend Goose when enemy deploys assassins to the Goose

:Composition:

   - 3 DPS

.. note::

   The **DPS** members include strategic and Twin Blade users as their skill sets are more suitable for skirmishes or small scale battle and single-target DPS.

Team 3 - Spec Ops
-----------------

:Objectives: The most disciplined team. Can be deployed anywhere. Carry out operations in hostile environments to secure strategic or operational advantages. Must be fast, efficient, and rotate wherever needed ASAP.

   - Clear out neutral camps, especially early in the game, to farm **Fun Coins**, the most valuable resource in GvG that are used to buy `Commander Buffs and Battlefield Items <cmdr_buffs.html>`_. The early economy lead gained from neutral camps will allow the Commander to level use important buffs faster.
   - After clearing neutral camps, they must rejoin the **Push Team** to defend the base in case of multiple invaders or rejoin **Main Force** to push, commit attack, or assassinate enemy healers from the back line
   - Terminating mini-bosses who gives big rewards. These bosses respawns at the 15 and 25 minute marks respectively. The first boss gives massive amount of **Fun Coins** (*around 3k, needs confirmation*) while the second boss gives buff called **Blood Nirvana**

   .. image:: /_static/{864797BA-4618-4382-AE42-41995718DE54}.png
         :align: center

   - (*optional for now*) One member must have **Ghost Bind** for fast camp clears; the earlier the camps are cleared the faster the Spec Ops can join other teams.

:Composition:

   - 3 DPS
